    Objective · wet well for LCU

    Slow to 5 kts or less first: the ship cannot ballast, recover or launch craft above that. Ballast down, lower the gate to 45° for the first water, open full at about 3 ft, and hold 8–10 ft at the gate with under 1° of list. Ballast only until the well reaches the depth you need, then put those tanks back in NORMAL. Secure a little early: the well keeps rising while valves close. Bring the LCU in, launch it, then deballast to a dry well. Expect a list as the well floods. Prestage the wing walls to 25–50% on both sides before you lower the gate: that leaves room to fill the high side, and water above sea level to drain from the low side.
